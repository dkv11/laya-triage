--- Error analysis: confidently wrong (team) ---
  0.92 "motor ka bearing itna garam ... jalan ho rahi hai" mechanical -> electrical
  0.91 "Motor chal raha hai but screen pe number ... kood raha hai" instrumentation -> electrical
  0.73 "motor ki base hil rahi hai ... bolts loose" mechanical -> electrical
  All 3 contain the word "motor" -> shortcut learned from the synthetic data:
  most "motor" messages were electrical (burning smell, humming, shock).
  Fix for v2: generate mechanical + instrumentation messages that mention the motor,
  and electrical messages that don't. Threshold cannot fix this; data can.
